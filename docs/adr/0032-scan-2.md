# ADR 0032 — Scan 2.0: automatic capture, batch review, Enhance, touch-up, ID card (P7a)

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-18

## Context

The v1 scan (ADR 0016) captures one page per shutter press, straightens it, offers three
filters and queues pages for one PDF. It left out automatic capture, shadow removal for colour
pages, editing a page after it was kept, and ID cards. P7a asks for all of them with the same
constraints: every pixel operation in `crates/selis-scan` (Rust → WebAssembly, imageproc, no new
non-permissive dependency), en + el strings, 44 pt targets, light/dark/sepia, reduced motion
respected, live detection ≤ 15 fps without per-frame allocations.

## Decision

### Automatic capture (`live.rs`)

- Each live frame (≤ 15 a second, ~320 px long side) goes through one export, `scan_live`:
  detection, then three measures — the **variance of the Laplacian** over the outline's
  bounding box (sharpness), the mean luma, the outline's share of the frame — and the
  `AutoCapture` tracker, which answers with a steady progress (0–1), "capture now" and a hint.
- **Fires** when the outline has held still for **0.6 s** — every corner moved less than
  **2 % of the page diagonal** (never less than 1.5 px: corners land on whole pixels) between
  consecutive frames — and the frame is **sharp** (≥ 800). The corners compared are each the
  median of the last three detections, and one frame without an outline is forgiven: on the
  emulator's TV the stand snaps a corner away now and then.
- **Calibration** on the scan-sample corpus (`packages/fixtures/scan`, `docs/store/scan-sample`)
  scaled to detection frames the way the WebView's `drawImage` scales the preview (bilinear, no
  area averaging; with area averaging the same photos measure 3–5× lower, so the scaling method
  is part of the threshold): sharp 2 699–7 590, out of focus (σ 1.5 px at 320 px) ≤ 256; 800 is
  ~3× from both. Tests replay every photo as 15 fps video (sub-pixel tremor, sensor noise): it
  fires once, 0.67 s in (1.7 s on the emulator TV); blurred or panning (3 % per frame) it never
  fires — in Rust (`samples.rs`) and through the built module in `packages/scan`
  (`auto.node.test.ts`, photos decoded by @napi-rs/canvas).
- After firing, the tracker stays quiet until the page changes: the outline is gone for 1 s (a
  page lifted and another put down; three lost frames re-armed it on the emulator under load),
  or (on a full three-frame median) moves more than 25 % of its diagonal — 10 % let the
  emulator TV's snapping stand corner re-arm it. The camera stops during Adjust; when it
  starts again (after Keep or Retake) the tracker *resumes* (`scan_auto_resume`): the page just
  captured is not taken again, and the camera's first empty frames do not count as the page
  going away (without this the emulator re-captured the same scene after every Keep). Switching
  Auto on, changing Document / ID card and Retake of a page from the review *reset* it
  (`scan_auto_reset`): the next steady page fires even if it is the same one.
- An automatic capture opens the Adjust step like a shutter press — except in a batch
  (amended 2026-10-19, below).
- **Hints** (en + el): "Hold steady" (outline not steady or not sharp), "Move closer" (outline
  < 35 % of the frame; it still fires — a small page is a page), "More light" (mean luma < 60;
  with a torch the hint turns it on).
- **UI**: a 56 px progress ring at the outline's centre fills with the steady progress (a
  stepped ring without transitions under reduced motion); a haptic on capture through the
  native view (`performHapticFeedback(CONFIRM)`, Android: no VIBRATE permission, follows the
  system's touch-feedback setting; nothing elsewhere). "Auto" toggle in the scan header,
  persisted as the setting `scanAuto` (default on). The shutter always works.
- **Allocations**: the frame's grey copy lives in the module between frames (reallocated only if
  the frame size changes), the WebAssembly frame buffer and the main thread's canvas are reused,
  and sharpness, luma and the tracker allocate nothing. Two allocations per frame remain outside
  our code: `getImageData` returns a new `ImageData` (the 2D canvas has no read-into API), and
  imageproc's Canny and contour tracing allocate their own buffers inside `detect` (unchanged
  from v1).

### Enhance filter (`enhance.rs`, the new default)

- The paper's brightness per channel is estimated on a copy box-averaged to 192 px (long side):
  a morphological close (max then min filter, radius 6, so ink up to ~6 % of the page goes),
  floored at 45 % of the paper's level (large dark areas — photos, filled boxes — are content,
  not shadow), blurred (σ 6). Each channel of the page is divided by it, interpolated per row;
  then levels 1–99 % (one curve for all channels) and an unsharp mask on the luma (3 × 3
  binomial, amount 0.8). Colour is kept. Three passes over the full page: ~170 ms on top of the
  warp for an A4 300 dpi page in Node (desktop).
- Measured on the generated photos (`samples.rs`, side margins of the straightened page; text
  contrast = mean 95th − 5th luma percentile of 32 px tiles with ink):

  | Photo | Background σ Original → Enhance | Text contrast |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | letter-wood | 20.6 → 1.5 (−93 %) | 174 → 241 |
  | letter-granite-steep (shadow 0.35) | 19.0 → 1.0 (−95 %) | 163 → 237 |
  | table-fabric-rotated | 15.0 → 1.7 (−89 %) | 178 → 243 |
  | table-lightgrey-lowcontrast | 9.3 → 1.5 (−84 %) | 178 → 236 |
  | receipt-wood (shadow 0.3) | 17.2 → 1.4 (−92 %) | 163 → 236 |
  | letter-fabric-shadow (shadow 0.55) | 48.5 → 2.5 (−95 %) | 143 → 235 |

  The test requires ≥ 50 % on the three shadowed photos and no contrast loss on any.
- Grey, Black & white and Original stay. Chips: Enhance, Grey, Black & white, Original.

### Page pipeline, touch-up, rotation (`render`, `touchup.rs`)

- A page is made from its photo and its edits: outline → straighten → filter → touch-up strokes
  → quarter turns (`scan_render`, which replaces `scan_straighten`). Strokes are stored relative
  to the straight page (points 0–1, radius as a share of the long side), so they survive a
  changed outline or output size.
- **Eraser**: each stroke's area (distance to the polyline ≤ radius) is filled with the
  per-channel median of a ring just outside it (width 0.6 × radius, ≥ 3 px), with a 1 px
  feathered edge. Undo removes the last stroke (the page is re-rendered from the photo).
- The photo stays with the page (the camera's JPEG, or a JPEG 0.92 the worker encodes when the
  still came as pixels), so any page can be reopened: corners, filter, rotation, touch-up.

### Batch and review

- After Keep the camera comes back; a fixed-height strip of thumbnails over the bottom of the
  preview (no layout shift) shows the pages with a count. The review screen (a grid) opens a
  page (re-adjust corners, filter, rotate 90°, touch up, retake, delete) and reorders by
  long-press drag (with move earlier / later buttons for keyboard and screen readers).
  Retake replaces the page in place. "Save" builds one PDF through `ImagePdfBuilder`
  (ADR 0017, "Fit image") and imports it as a new library document.
- **Memory cap**: pages are blobs in the WebView (photo + page + thumbnail, ~3–8 MB each). The
  batch holds at most 120 / 240 / 480 MB on the low / mid / high tier (`limits.ts`); at the cap
  the shutter stops and a sheet offers to save (or review).

### ID card mode

- A "Document / ID card" switch on the camera (while the batch is empty). ID card: two
  captures, front then back (hints say which), default filter Original (Enhance would flatten
  the card's printed background). Save places both on one A4 page (595.28 × 841.89 pt) at the
  ID-1 size, 85.6 × 54 mm (landscape; 54 × 85.6 for a portrait side), centred one above the
  other with 10 mm between, through a new `ImagePdfSession.addSheet` (several images at given
  boxes on one page; JPEGs embedded as they are).

## Consequences

- WebAssembly size: SIMD 179 262 → 212 954 bytes (gzip 67 317 → 79 670), base 172 778 →
  201 167 bytes. JS 372 086 → 379 687 bytes gzip (budget 1.5 MB). No new crate; `cargo deny`
  unchanged.
- One new native command (`scan_haptic`, Android only) and one new setting (`scanAuto`, no
  schema change: settings are key–value).
- Not done: haptics on iOS (no native code there yet), inpainting beyond the ring median (a
  stroke across a photo on the page fills it flat).

## Amendment (2026-10-19): automatic Keep in a batch; the camera header at 360 dp

- **Auto-keep.** An automatic capture is kept at once — no Adjust step — when Auto is on, the
  session is a document batch (at least one page kept; there is no separate batch switch), and
  the detection is confident: the live outline's weakest side lies on edge pixels for ≥ 85 % of
  its middle 80 % (`live::AUTO_KEEP_SUPPORT`, stricter than the 70 % any found outline needs;
  `scan_live` now also returns this support), and the page is found in the still — by
  `find_page`, which now tries a bilinear-sampled copy when the area-averaged one finds nothing
  (the emulator's TV bezel vanished in the latter), or, when the still has the live frame's
  shape, the live outline scaled. It is kept with the default filter (Enhance) and that outline. The camera shows "Page N added"
  for 600 ms (announced to screen readers) and the thumbnail flies from the middle of the
  preview into the strip (400 ms; none with reduced motion); the tracker then re-arms by the
  existing rule (the page gone 1 s, or moved 25 %). The first page, a low-confidence outline,
  a page not found in the still, a retake, ID card mode and the manual shutter open Adjust as
  before (also the still found no page and has another shape). Every photo of the scan-sample
  corpus held steady has support ≥ 0.91 (`samples.rs`).
- **Header.** The camera header keeps its text 16 dp from the screen edges at 360 dp in en and
  el: no page count before the first page (the strip and badge show it after), the title
  truncates with an ellipsis, the count is capped at 96 px. Checked by a Playwright layout test
  and 360 × 800 visual baselines (`scan-header-360-*`).
